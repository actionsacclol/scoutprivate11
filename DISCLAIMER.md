## The models lose to the closing line

This is the honest part most tools hide, so we put it in the disclaimer:

- The bundled v1 models are calibrated Elo/Poisson baselines. In backtests, the
  **devigged closing line beats every one of them** on log loss.
- A model that can't beat the close has **no proven betting edge**, whatever its
  raw accuracy number looks like. That is exactly why Krypt Scout ships CLV
  tracking — to measure the gap instead of pretending it isn't there.
- Backtest accuracy, edge percentages, EV figures, and Kelly stakes are model
  outputs under model assumptions. They carry **no guarantee of profitability**.
- Past record — the model's or yours — does not indicate future results.

If you bet every edge the value board shows at full Kelly, expect to lose money.
